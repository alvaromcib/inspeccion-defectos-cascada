Detect, track and decide: automatic steel strip inspection on CPU

A YOLO11 detector analyses every frame of a hot-rolled strip, tracks each defect as the strip moves and produces a per-section report with an explicit decision: OK, review or reject. No GPU, and no images leave the plant.

The system

Three simple, measurable steps. Each one can be tuned with plant data.

Step 1 · every frame

YOLO11n detector

Localises and classifies defects into six types. Lightweight on purpose: it runs on a line PC with no GPU.

→
Step 2 · tracking

One defect, one object

Links detections across frames using the strip position (the line encoder). The type is decided by majority.

→
Step 3 · per section

Report and decision

Defects and types per strip section, with an explicit, configurable quality rule.

Discarded experiment

A vision-language model as a second stage

A cascade was tested in which a local VLM (Qwen2.5-VL with Ollama) reviewed crops of each defect to classify it, grade its severity and explain it. Measured on the same defects, it made the system worse.

With plant data

Find more defectsThe demo detector localises half of the labelled defects. With the plant's own images, higher resolution and more examples of the hard types, this is the first thing to improve.
A real quality ruleThe rule in this demo is an example. In a plant the quality manager sets it per product and customer, and it is validated against the history of rejections.
Line synchronisationA line-scan or area camera synchronised with the encoder, to place each defect in metres along the coil.
Integration and traceabilityA report per coil or batch in the MES, with the image of each defect, without sending video outside the plant.

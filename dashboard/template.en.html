Why a cascade

CostA VLM on every frame is expensive and slow. Here it only sees a few crops per defect.
ReliabilityConsensus across answers filters out erratic VLM outputs, and the VLM can discard detector false positives.
PrivacyThe VLM runs locally with Ollama: plant images never leave the factory.
ExplainableEach defect carries a type, a severity and a natural-language reason for the quality report.
